# Neocloud Guide coverage audit, October 2026

This audit compares the Neocloud Guide with the companies a knowledgeable reader would expect to find on 2026-10-14. It starts from objective lists (GetDeploying's H100, B200 and B300 price pages and its provider index, NVIDIA cloud partner announcements, and 2025 and 2026 press lists of neoclouds) and then checks each category by hand. Fal is out of scope because it is researched separately. The guide's standalone repo at /Users/sonne/Documents/Github/neoclouds now lists 87 entries including Fal.

The guide covers Alibaba Cloud but no other Chinese cloud, no accelerator-maker cloud besides Groq, no decentralized GPU network and no North American telco AI cloud. Riot and CleanSpark, two large miners with signed AI leases, are also missing while all their peers are in.

Must-have means a knowledgeable reader would notice the gap. Should-have means the company fits the guide and has real scale, but its absence is less obvious. Optional means the company is small, early, regional, or only partly a compute seller.

Every URL in this report appeared in a search result or was fetched during the audit. A cell marked (search result) points to a page that appeared in search results but blocked fetching, so its facts come from the search snippet. The session hit its cap of 200 web searches partway through. A few agents briefly used a Google News RSS script after the cap before I withdrew it, so a small number of URLs come from that script's results. The last section lists what the audit did not reach.
